# Design System: Neuebit public landing

Scope: public marketing only. The user's final locked brief overrides generic skill defaults (including single-accent, spring and perpetual-animation defaults). Global theme tokens remain owned by `src/styles/theme.css`; authenticated layouts and actions are outside this document's scope.

## 1. Visual theme and atmosphere

Editorial knowledge product first, precise vector machinery underneath. Calm, friendly, product-led, restrained personality. Original document/search/source/node motifs; the duck is a small brand mark. Density increases in the engineering chapter, without changing themes against the visitor's choice.

## 2. Color palette and roles

Canvas White `#FFFFFF`, Neutral Field `#F7F7F5`, Ink `#191919`, Secondary `#5F5E5B`, Default Border `#E3E3E0` define light. Deep Canvas `#08090A`, Field `#0D0E10`, Surface `#111214`, Elevated `#17181A`, Text `#F4F4F5`, Secondary `#A1A1AA`, white at 9% border define dark.

Action Blue `#176FCA` is the sole primary action, with white text. Soft Action Blue `#E7F3FF` and Blue Ink `#165DA6` form the secondary GitHub action. Documents blue `#4C8DFF`, Search coral `#F06A4F`, Chat green `#35B779`, Vector violet `#8B6FE8`, technical yellow `#E8B84A` are small semantic accents. Dark accents are muted and their tinted surfaces occupy less visual area. See `frontend/MARKETING.md` for exact fill/ink roles.

## 3. Typography

Locally hosted Geist Sans for headlines/body; Geist Mono for machine labels and values. The centered hero H1 uses `clamp(2.625rem, calc(11.25vw - 25.5px), 6rem)`, 700 weight and responsive leading capped at 100px. Desktop type caps at 96px; container-based limits keep enlarged text and the inline phrase within their measure. Static "Your knowledge," is followed by a soft capsule and static "in context." The capsule cycles Ask questions / Search meaning / Trace answers / Inspect retrieval at 75% of the surrounding headline size, with regular 400-weight black ink, a .3em semantic dot, 8px/28px padding, no border and no shadow. The capsule stays pale in both modes to preserve black-text contrast; dark mode uses the existing muted accent hues. An invisible shared grid slot reserves the longest phrase. Each visible capsule fits its own text, aligns beside the static suffix on desktop, and centers on mobile; the surrounding headline remains fixed. Only the active phrase is accessible. The separate rail below the CTAs is removed. Mobile places the phrase and "in context." on consistent separate rows; narrow/enlarged-text measures reduce capsule padding to 16px and adapt display type. Body text retains its enlarged size. Supporting copy stays centered at 20px/1.4, bounded to 680px. No per-letter animation or decorative serif.

## 4. Component styling

Flat primary/soft-blue secondary actions, visible keyboard focus and 44px mobile controls. Product surfaces use 1px borders and 6–12px radii. Source and selected-row tints communicate state. Shared job glyphs retain their small original SVG geometries and fine rounded outlines. Discovery has independent mini-posters: a question and document, a semantic search lens, an answer connected back to its passage, and a vector projection. Their 144 × 120 SVG viewboxes render at 96 × 80px, with 2.25px rounded ink strokes and asymmetric organic fields. Each card uses exactly one accent color, alongside neutral ink and paper: Ask blue `#4E91EF`, Search coral `#EB765D`, Trace green `#43AD7D`, Inspect violet `#9274D6`. Dark accents use `#608DBA`, `#C8816C`, `#639B80` and `#947CAE` respectively. Fields and passage highlights share the same accent; connectors and outline nodes are neutral. Card surfaces remain neutral. These scoped colors do not alter the product stories or global palette. Architecture uses a compact process rail, changing machine canvas and trace console; never seven generic feature cards. Final CTA and footer form one compact closing wrapper with a full-viewport footer border, bounded content and real destinations. No closing illustration or giant ghost wordmark.

## 6. Motion and interaction

Existing Framer Motion only. Tween easing `cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)`, page reveals 500–650ms, hero product entrance 800ms, local content changes ~300ms, hover/focus 150–300ms. The H1 phrase cycles every 3 seconds, independently of the unchanged product film. Phrase opacity and y8 settle over 350ms; capsule tint and dot color crossfade over 350ms; phrase ink stays black. Hidden/offscreen time suspends phrase changes; reduced motion renders static Ask questions immediately. The actual ~15s product film autoplays muted inline with native looping and no visible controls, Replay or timeline UI. Offscreen/hidden documents suspend playback. Reduced motion shows a theme-matched static poster without downloading a movie. Film ends on the exact opening Documents frame. The retrieval explanation now reuses five authored artifacts through one reversible, scroll-controlled scene in a 180svh wrapper: source material first, then the question and semantic match, selected passage, answer, and attached citation. Existing source and answer copy remains unchanged. No permanent pipeline competes with the active artifact. During the last approximately 15% those same elements form an unboxed full-system overview, then release naturally. A passive scroll listener writes MotionValues in requestAnimationFrame; React updates only at phase, completion or eligibility boundaries. No timer, replay, looping motion or added dependency. Architecture loops locally with 2s stage dwell and a 5.6s rest after returning to Ask. Clicking or using the keyboard to select a stage stops autoplay until explicit Resume. Pause/Resume is available without Replay. Offscreen/hidden time is excluded; a live reduced-motion preference renders a stable interactive stage. The marquee retains hover/focus/manual pause.

The retrieval scene uses a shared horizontal artifact band with one focal object and its quiet causal predecessor. A blue document fragment, compact violet semantic neighborhood with a coral selection, selected passage, neutral answer and green citation all retain the same DOM identity as they move into the final five-stage composition. The final overview measures up to 1248px across a 352px scene; each stage has an original small glyph, a 22px heading and one short supporting value, without a conventional card. Connectors draw between the actual moving objects. Source provenance is a shallow 0.75px green trace, drawn right to left; the final annotation reads “Citation preserved. Follow it back to the original passage.” Only the completed state enables Document/Passage/Source hover and keyboard focus to strengthen their relevant relationships, with visible outlines and no unrelated dimming. Below 52rem of available content width, the same five artifacts stack in natural reading order with full explanatory copy and short vertical connectors. Mobile has no fixed scene height or pinning. Short viewports and enlarged text also return to complete natural flow whenever the content cannot fit below the navbar. Reduced motion renders the completed state immediately, with no scroll transforms or drawing. No nested scroll container, scroll snapping, progress tracker or horizontal page scrolling.

The vector discovery drawing uses one violet circular field, four neighboring nodes and one selected node within a compact connected graph. Rounded arrowheads finish both positive axes. Seven carefully separated edges restore the original network character without axis ticks, projection clutter or overlapping selection rings. It retains the existing 96 × 80 display dimensions and single accent color.

Discovery hover and keyboard focus share a 200ms eased response: a 3px card lift, 3px arrow shift, 1–2px illustration offsets, connector completion and a vector focus-dot fade. Color and title positions remain stable. Shadows are very light in light mode and absent in dark mode. There are no repeating pulses or new motion dependencies. Reduced motion keeps all geometry still and connectors complete, while retaining border and focus feedback.

## 7. Anti-patterns

No copied reference layouts/illustrations, large mascot, fake social proof or post-V1 claims. No cream canvas, navy dark cast, neon, glows, glass, large shadows, noisy gradients, custom cursor, scroll-jacking, autoplay scrolling, decorative loops or arbitrary AI input. No remote demo-state controls. No AppShell, D3, product polling or new animation dependency on public entry. No authenticated redesign. No commit or push until visual review.
